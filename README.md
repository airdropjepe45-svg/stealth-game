The Gilded Vault — a top-down 2D stealth game in a night museum. Wren the thief sneaks through three wings, stays out of the guards' vision cones, hides behind crates and in bushes, and reaches the green exit unseen. The final wing holds the Star Sapphire.

## How to play

- **Move** with WASD or the arrow keys. Hold **Shift** to sneak: slower, quieter, and harder for guards to spot.
- **Click** anywhere nearby to throw a pebble (3 per level). Guards within earshot walk over to investigate the sound — use it to slip past a patrol.
- **Vision cones** show exactly what a guard can see; walls and crates block sight. A yellow `?` means a guard is suspicious and coming to look. A red `!` means you have been spotted — run.
- **Bushes** hide you unless a guard is almost on top of you. **Shadows** slow down how fast a guard's detection meter fills.
- **The Garden Wing** teaches the basics. **The Hall of Kings** hides the Golden Key behind a patrol — take it before the exit opens. **The Vault** holds the Star Sapphire under four guards. Finish a wing without ever being spotted for a GHOST rating.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. WASD or arrows to move, Shift to sneak, click to throw a pebble that distracts a guard.
